# «القطة اللي مو قطة!» — برومتات الأصوات

## وصف الأصوات
- **ترف:** Young Saudi girl, about 7 years old. Bright, playful and cheeky voice, energetic, giggles easily. Saudi dialect.
- **سحاب:** Young Saudi girl, about 6-7 years old. Excited, a little bossy and stubborn, but caring and brave when it matters. Saudi dialect.
- **ملاذ:** Young Saudi girl, about 7 years old. Sensible and cautious, slightly lower and softer voice, gets breathy and whispery when scared. Saudi dialect.
- **لين:** Very young Saudi girl, about 4-5 years old, the youngest. Tiny, cute, high-pitched voice, a little babyish, easily frightened. Saudi dialect.
- **فارس:** Young Saudi boy, about 7 years old. Energetic, adventurous, loud and fast-talking. Saudi dialect.
- **جسار:** Saudi boy, about 10 years old, older than the others. Confident, calm and brave voice, slightly deeper than the younger kids. Saudi dialect.
